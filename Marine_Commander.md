# <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Natural Selection 2</span>

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Marine Commander — Decision Guide</span>

Protect your team's income, help Marines win useful fights, and make a plan to win. Tell the team what to do and where to go.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">How to Use This Guide</span>

- Follow the opening and upgrade order, but keep watching the map and helping Marines.
- Times are goals. Change your plan when resources, Marine positions, or Alien threats change.
- Keep the Lerk and Fade checks running after each is first seen and confirmed.
- Use the **Push Checklist** before a normal push and the **ARC Push Checklist** before an ARC push.
- **IF** important information is missing: ask Field Marines or scan when needed. **ELSE:** use what you know to choose the next action.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Short Names and Key Terms</span>

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| TRES | Team resources. Used for buildings, research, and commander support. |
| PRES | Personal resources. Marines use these to buy weapons and gear. |
| RT | Resource Tower. Gives your team income. |
| IP | Infantry Portal. Where Marines respawn. |
| PG | Phase Gate. Lets Marines travel quickly between gates. |
| AA | Advanced Armory. |
| ARC | A mobile siege weapon used to attack Alien buildings. |
| HMG | Heavy Machine Gun. |
| GL | Grenade Launcher. |
| W1 / W2 / W3 | Weapons 1 / Weapons 2 / Weapons 3 upgrades. |
| A1 / A2 / A3 | Armor 1 / Armor 2 / Armor 3 upgrades. |
| ASAP | As soon as the needed research is done and you have enough TRES. |
| Field Marine | A teammate playing on the map, outside the Command Station. |
| Powerbuild | Opening where a teammate builds the Arms Lab for you. |
| Lifeforms | In the Lerk and Fade checks, this means Lerks and Fades. Watch for other threats, such as Onos, too. |
| Alien group / pack | Aliens moving or fighting together. |
| Push | A planned team attack on a target. |
| Beacon | Calls Marines back to defend the base. Keep the required TRES ready. |

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 1 — Game Start</span>

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">IF POWERBUILD = True</span>

1. Place the Arms Lab in the middle of base, where Marines can see it clearly.
2. Start **Armor 1** research ASAP.
3. Place both starting RTs near base.
4. Leave the Command Station.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">ELSE — Build It Yourself</span>

1. Place the Arms Lab in a safe spot near the Command Station.
2. Place both starting RTs near base.
3. Leave the Command Station and build the Arms Lab yourself.
4. Enter the Command Station again.
5. Start **Armor 1** research ASAP.
6. Leave the Command Station.

**After either path:** Play normally as a Field Marine, follow the next steps.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 2 — Early Resources and Upgrades</span>

DISCLAIMER: YOU ARE STILL RESPONSIBLE FOR COMMANDER RESEARCH AND BUILDING EVEN AS YOU PLAY IN THE FIELD.

Play as a Field Marine. Watch TRES and where your team is. **Ask teammates** who are spawning to place buildings or start research in this order. 

| Order | Action | When / where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Armor 1 | Start ASAP in Phase 1. **IF** already started or done: skip this step. **ELSE:** start it now. |
| 2 | Second Infantry Portal | **IF** the second IP is already placed: skip this step. **ELSE IF** TRES is 20 or more: ask for it now. **ELSE:** wait for 20 TRES. |
| 3 | RTs near the middle of the map | **IF** Marines can take the area and build: place the RTs. **ELSE:** wait for Marines to be ready. |
| 4 | Weapons 1 | Start after Armor 1 is done, as soon as you have enough TRES. |
| 5 | Armory | Between both IPs, near the Command Station. |
| 6 | Observatory | In a safe spot. Try to cover one or more base entrances. |
| 7 | Phase Gate research | Start ASAP. |
| 8 | Armor 2 | Start saving TRES as soon as Weapons 1 is done. Start Armor 2 as soon as you have enough TRES. |

**IMPORTANT** Bind Observarory to quick access with ctrl + 1 for fast beaconing in case of a base rush.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 3 — First Lerk / Return to Command</span>

**When the first Lerk is seen and confirmed (around minute 3):**

1. Return to the **Command Station** ASAP.
2. Run **Lerk Check** on repeat.
3. Give Medpacks using the **General Medpack and Support Guidelines** below.

**While commanding against Lerks:** watch key fights. Track the Alien group and try to predict where it will attack next.

**Plan for this:** Lerks are very important to the Alien team at this point. Expect Aliens to attack RTs with few Marines nearby, one at a time.

## <span style="color: #d99119;">Lerk Check — Are the Lerks Dead?</span>

**Start when the first Lerk is seen and confirmed. Keep this check running for the rest of the game.** Keep upgrades going while you respond to fights.

Track how many Lerks are alive. Update the count when a Lerk dies or a new Lerk is seen for sure.

- **IF** living Lerks > 0: keep playing as normal. Watch key fights, track the Alien group, and try to predict its next attack.
- **ELSE IF** living Lerks = 0: go to the **Fade Check** below.
- **ELSE** (Lerk count is not known): get updated information before calling a push.

**Remember:** One dead Lerk does not mean all Lerks are dead. Make sure to get an accurate head count before decidng a push.

**No living Lerks may give your team a short time to attack. This does not mean you should push yet.** Check Fades next. Also consider whether Aliens can evolve into new Lerks or Fades before your team reaches the target.

## <span style="color: #23a89b;">General Medpack and Support Guidelines</span>

Before giving support, ask:

- **Can these Marines kill an Alien lifeform with support?**
- **Are they defending an important building, such as a Phase Gate or Resource Tower?**
- **Does the Marine have valuable equipment, such as a Shotgun, HMG, or Jetpack?**
- **Are other Marines coming to help?**

- **IF** at least one answer is **YES:** give support while it can still help the team.
- **ELSE** (all answers are **NO**): stop support now. Save resources for more useful fights.

**Keep checking during the fight.** A useful fight can become a waste of resources.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 5 — First Fades</span>

**Expected time in this plan:** about **6–7 minutes**.

**When the first Fade is seen and confirmed, do these steps right away:**

1. Save **20 TRES for Shotgun research**. Start research as soon as you have enough TRES.
2. Ask Field Marines to group up and defend the **Phase Gates**.
3. Play defensively until Marines have Shotguns. Expect Aliens to attack a PG hard before Shotguns are ready.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Scans and Support</span>

**Commander support is very important now.** Keep following the **General Medpack and Support Guidelines**.

- **IF** you need to find the Alien group: use a scan. Use a second scan only if you still need more information.
- **ELSE:** save the scan.

**Keep Shotgun research first.** Do not delay it by spending too much on Medpacks or other support. Keep checking whether support can still help the team.

## <span style="color: #d99119;">Fade Check — Are the Fades Dead?</span>

**Start when the first Fade is seen and confirmed. Keep this check running for the rest of the game.** Keep upgrades going while you respond to fights.

Track how many Fades are alive. Update the count when a Fade dies or a new Fade is seen and confirmed.

- **IF** living Fades > 0: keep playing as normal. Watch key fights, track the Alien group, and try to predict its next attack.
- **ELSE IF** living Fades = 0: go to the **Push Checklist** below.
- **ELSE** (Fade count is not known): get updated information before calling a push.

**Remember:** One dead Fade does not mean all Fades are dead. Make sure to get an accurate head count before deciding a push.

**No living Fades may give your team a short time to attack. This does not mean you should push yet.** Use the Push Checklist next. Also consider whether Aliens can evolve into new Lerks or Fades before your team reaches the target.

---

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Push Checklist</span>

**Check all of these before calling a push. Few living Lerks or Fades alone is not enough.**

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">1. Living Lerks and Fades</span>

- **IF** no more than **1 Lerk and 1 Fade** are alive (**2 total at most**): continue the checklist.
- **ELSE:** wait. **IF** the count is not known: get updated information first.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">2. Resources for Support and Beacon</span>

- **IF** you have enough TRES for support **and can keep at least 10 TRES for Beacon**: continue the checklist.
- **ELSE:** save more TRES before calling the push.

**Keep the 10 TRES ready for Beacon.** Use the General Medpack and Support Guidelines when spending on the push.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">3. Resource Towers</span>

- **IF** Marines have **fewer than 3 RTs:** take back RTs first, then check the push again.
- **ELSE:** continue the checklist.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">4. Marine Positions</span>

Check where Marines are on the map. Tell them where to meet and which path to take.

- **IF** Marines can group up and attack together: continue the checklist.
- **ELSE:** get Marines into position first.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">5. Target — Most Important Check</span>

**Name the target. Can Marines win this fight with their current weapons and upgrades?** W1 = Weapons 1; W2 = Weapons 2.

- **IF** there is no clear target: do not call the push.
- **ELSE:** check the target below.

| Target | Check before attacking |
| --- | --- |
| Fully built Hive | **IF** Weapons 2 is done: continue the checklist. **ELSE:** cancel this push. |
| Hive still building or just built | **IF** Weapons 1 or higher is done: continue the checklist. **ELSE:** cancel this push. |
| Important room, such as Nanogrid | Usually possible at any upgrade level. **IF** Marines can reach the room quickly: continue the checklist. **ELSE:** plan faster access before calling the push. |

**These are the minimum upgrade checks.** Also check whether the Marines going to the target can win the fight there.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">6. Base and PG Defense</span>

**Order one Marine to stay back and defend the base / PG.** Aliens may rush the base after losing most of their Lerks and Fades.

- **IF** one Marine is ready to defend and Beacon is ready to use: finish the checklist.
- **ELSE:** set up defense before calling the push.

**Stay ready to Beacon Marines back if the base needs help.**

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">7. Check the Time and Keep Watching</span>

Allow time for Marines to group up and reach the target. Consider whether Alien players could evolve into new Lerks or Fades before Marines arrive. Later in the game, they may already have enough resources.

- **IF** all checks pass and the attack still looks possible when Marines arrive: call the push.
- **ELSE:** wait or change the plan, then check again.

**Keep checking during the push:** living Lerks and Fades, Marine positions, TRES, the target, and base safety.

- **IF** the situation changes: check the push again. Stop or change the attack when needed.
- **ELSE:** keep going and keep watching.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 6 — Mid to Late Game Upgrade Order</span>

**Keep research going while you command and support Marines.** These times are goals.

| Order | Upgrade | When / cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Shotguns | Around **6 minutes**, or when Fades are seen and confirmed. Follow the First Fades section when they appear. |
| 2 | Weapons 2 | Usually right after Shotguns. Start saving TRES when Shotgun research starts. |
| 3 | Advanced Armory | Start while Weapons 2 is researching. **25 TRES**. |
| 4 | Jetpacks | Aim for **10–12 minutes**. **40 TRES**. Check Marine PRES first using the rule below. |
| 5 | Weapons 3 | Save toward this while Jetpacks are researching, or research it first when Marines cannot afford Jetpack gear. **40 TRES**. |
| 6 | Armor 3 | Late-game upgrade to help counter the Fade's **Advanced Swipe**. |

- **IF** an upgrade is already done or researching: move to the next step.
- **ELSE:** start it when the needed research is done and you have enough TRES.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Jetpacks or Weapons 3 — Check PRES First</span>

**Check the Field Marines' PRES before choosing Jetpacks or Weapons 3.** Marines need their own PRES to buy gear, even after the team pays TRES for research.

- **Jetpack:** 15 PRES.
- **Shotgun, HMG, Flamethrower, or Grenade Launcher:** 20 PRES each.
- **Jetpack + one of these weapons:** **35 PRES per Marine**.

- **AIM** for general Marine PRES of at least **40** before Jetpack research.
- **ELSE** research **Weapons 3 and support upgrades** instead. 
- **Check** PRES again before researching Jetpacks later.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 8 — ARCs or Jetpacks?</span>

**Choose how to attack the Hive:**

**Prefer a Jetpack AA push when all of these are true:**

- Aliens have very few or no living lifeforms.
- Marine PRES meets the Phase 6 guidelines for Jetpack gear.
- Jetpacks and the weapons needed for the push are ready.
- All checks in the **Push Checklist** pass, including the Hive upgrade check, support TRES, Marine positions, and base defense.

- **IF** all of these are true: plan a **Jetpack AA push**, even when the Hive can also be hit by ARCs.
- **ELSE IF** ARCs can reliably hit the Hive from a position Marines can hold: plan an **ARC push** on that Hive.
- **ELSE:** continue the normal research path as stated in **Phase 6**

**When the Jetpack push checks do not pass, favor ARCs instead of Jetpack research when all of these are true:**

- Aliens have many living lifeforms.
- The map has a Hive that ARCs can reliably hit.
- Your other upgrades are on time.

**ARCs are an especially good choice when Aliens have no Shade Hive.** Build them in a safe place.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Examples of Hives That ARCs Can Reliably Hit</span>

| Map | Hive / ARC position |
| --- | --- |
| Tram | Hit **Warehouse** from **North Tunnels**. |
| Summit | With **Sub-Sector** and **Data Core** Hives, ARC from **Computer Lab**. |
| Veil | Hit **Pipeline** from **The Neck**. |
| Kodiak | Hit **Command** from **Southern Biosphere** or **Hydroponics**.  |
| Jambi | Hit **Waste Recycling** from **Bay Wing**. |
| Ayumi | Hit **AE35** from **Monitoring**. |

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Build ARCs Near the Front</span>

You do not need to build ARCs at Marine start. You can hide an **Advanced Robotics Factory** near a PG and build ARCs closer to the target. Choose a safe spot.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Use One ARC While Building More</span>

On some maps, one ARC can keep an Alien RT down while you build more ARCs.

Examples:

| Map | Alien RT | ARC position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jambi | **Organ Donation** | **Gravity** |
| Tram | **Mezzanine** | **Elevator** |
| Veil | **Nanogrid** | **East Junction** or **West Junction** |
| Eclipse | **Generator Monitors** | **Conduit** |
| Eclipse | **South Loop** | **T-Junction** |

**ARC pushes use a different checklist from normal pushes.** See the next section.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 9 — ARC Push Checklist</span>

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">1. ARCs and Marine Gear</span>

- **IF** Aliens have a **Shade Hive:** bring at least **4 ARCs**.
- **ELSE:** bring at least **2 ARCs**.

**Before starting the push:**

- **Advanced Armory (AA)** must be complete.
- Marines must have enough PRES to buy **1 Flamethrower** and at least **2 Shotguns and/or HMGs** in total.

- **IF** the required ARCs and Marine gear are ready: set up the forward base.
- **ELSE:** prepare them before starting the push.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">2. Forward Base</span>

Build a forward base near the target Hive with at least:

- **1 Phase Gate**.
- **1 Observatory in range of the Hive**.

- **IF** Aliens have **Onos:** place an **Armory** to block the closest entrance from the Hive into your ARC room.
- **ELSE:** this Armory block is not required.

**Keep at least 10 TRES ready for Beacon throughout the push.** Be ready to bring Marines back if your base is rushed.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">3. Keep ARCs Firing</span>

Keep ARCs firing at useful targets.

- **IF** Alien defenses or upgrade structures are in range: hit those first.
- **ELSE:** fire at the Hive.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">4. Protect Your Income</span>

**ARC pushes can take a long time when both teams play well.** Watch TRES and your RTs throughout the push.

Both teams may get stuck fighting over the same area. Small fights for RTs around the map often decide who can keep going. Keep sending Marines to take and defend RTs.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">5. Watch the Alien Group and the Hive</span>

Track where the Alien group is and how many Aliens are defending the Hive.

- **IF** you see a good chance to attack: send Marines into the Hive.
- **ELSE:** keep the ARC pressure going and watch for a chance.

**Few Aliens defending the Hive may mean a base rush is coming.** Keep checking your base and be ready to Beacon Marines back.

## <span style="color: #3b82f6;">Phase 10 — General Commander Gameplay Loop</span>

**Protect your team's income while hurting Alien income.** Keep repeating these checks and actions as the game changes.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Protect RTs and Attack Alien RTs</span>

Balance defense and attack. Give Marines routes to watch so Aliens cannot move freely through your side of the map.

A common plan is:

- Marines with **Shotguns** hold PGs and RTs. Use hidden positions to surprise Aliens.
- Marines with **Rifles** attack Alien RTs and keep pressure on Alien income.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Track Lifeforms and Set Traps</span>

Use scans and Field Marine reports to track the Alien lifeform group. Tell the team where Aliens are moving.

Work with Field Marines to trap lifeforms that overextend themselves. Ask Marines to rotate to fights as they happen to catch Aliens on the retreat.

**Killing strong lifeforms creates chances to win.** Give Marines the support they need to get those kills, then use the opening with your team.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Keep Marines Alive and Protect the Base</span>

Give Medpacks and other support using the **General Medpack and Support Guidelines**.

**Jetpack Marines are a top support priority in every fight, especially against lifeforms.** Keep them alive while also watching for a base rush.

- **IF** Aliens are crushing a PG and your commander support is not urgently needed: you may leave the Command Station to help defend. **Pulse Grenades** can be especially useful.
- **ELSE:** stay in the Command Station and keep supporting Marines.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Choose a Plan and Tell the Team</span>

Compare your team's income and upgrades with Alien income, upgrades, and living lifeforms. Choose a plan that fits the current game:

- **Hunt lifeforms:** work with Marines to trap and kill strong Aliens.
- **Defend and research:** hold safe positions, protect income, and get the upgrades you need.
- **Group up and push:** bring Marines together and use the right push checklist.

**Tell the team what the plan is and where Marines need to go.**

- **IF** the game changes: check your plan and change it when needed. Tell the team the new plan.
- **ELSE:** keep working toward the current plan.

### <span style="color: #23a89b;">Keep This Loop Running</span>

1. Track lifeforms and call out Alien movements.
2. Keep Marines alive with Medpacks and support.
3. Watch your base and protect your income.
4. Have a plan to win and tell the team what it is.
